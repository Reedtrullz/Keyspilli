# Sung voice → piano melody

Recover the sung line first; arrange it for piano separately. Raw pYIN F0 frames and Basic Pitch events are hypotheses, not an accepted melody. Do not repair a failed vocal line by changing its octave or deleting short notes alone.

## Bounded diagnosis

Pin the requested recording and actual stem bytes, separator version, sample rate, frame/hop/centering, crop offset and native clock. Compare identical source-mix/stem excerpts across the first entry, fast syllabic verse, repeated attacks, sustained chorus/ending, instrumental intro and spoken material. Agreeing trackers can track bleed and speech. ASR word boundaries locate possible syllables, not necessarily musical onsets. Check what the stem removed as well as what it retained.

Compare continuous F0 before rounding: octave jumps, pitch dispersion, tuning residuals, vibrato/slides, onset consonants, vowel centres and releases. Mark unstable/spoken passages unresolved. A global tuning offset needs consistent evidence across stable notes; do not centre each phrase independently. Check missing syllables/attacks, merged repeats, holes inside sustains and real rests. Never replace unvoiced NaNs with a plausible pitch before smoothing.

| Approach | Use | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| [pYIN](https://librosa.org/doc/0.11.0/generated/librosa.pyin.html) | Continuous F0/voicing baseline | Range/confidence/eventization can erase attacks or fragment gestures |
| [CREPE / torchcrepe](https://github.com/maxrmorrison/torchcrepe) | Complementary pitch hypothesis; inspect periodicity/octaves | No note-boundary model; can track silence/noise; Viterbi penalizes jumps |
| [Basic Pitch](https://github.com/spotify/basic-pitch) | Onset/release and bend candidates on a stem | Polyphonic harmonics/adjacent bins must not become piano attacks automatically |
| [ROSVOT](https://github.com/RickyL-2000/ROSVOT) | Singing-specific boundaries and optional word alignment | Published checkpoint trained on Mandarin; other languages need local evaluation |
| [STARS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.06670) | Joint note/word/phoneme/style research | Not a piano arranger or listening gate |

Use installed working inference paths first. Add a heavyweight singing model only for a measured failure. Record actual versions/settings and controls; agreement is not ground-truth accuracy.

## Recovery packet

`python3 scripts/recover_vocal.py PACKET.json --source ACTUAL_RECORDING` checks source bytes against the packet hash and emits provisional pitches inside supplied native-time attack hypotheses. It does not discover boundaries, classify speech, certify evidence or choose piano phrasing. Preserve the packet and tracker assets. Do not label a passage singing solely from voiced/periodic confidence.

Strict packet fields: `schemaVersion: 1`, `sourceSha256`, `clock: "native-seconds"`, `tuningCents`, `tuningBasis`, `trackers`, `attacks`.

- Tracker: distinct `method`, reported `threshold`, ordered `frames: [[nativeSeconds, midiFloatOrNull, confidence], ...]`. Different models' confidences are not calibrated equivalents.
- Attack: unique `id`, exact `start`, `end`, `kind` (`singing`, `speech`, `bleed`, `unknown`), substantive `boundaryEvidence`. Split repeats only with source attack evidence; join ties only with source support. Preserve rests. Keep slide/vibrato samples within a gesture rather than attacking every rounded semitone.
- Optional paired `pitchWindow: [nativeStart, nativeEnd]` and `pitchEvidence` identify a source-supported vowel core **inside** that attack. Estimate pitch there while preserving the full attack/release. The helper also reports whole-gesture pitch spread, support and core fraction so a small core cannot conceal poor coverage. Do not select a convenient core to turn a melisma into one note, hide an octave disagreement or justify a long hold. Core selection requires source evidence; the helper does not choose it automatically.
- The helper flags unsupported, between-key, wide-spread and disagreeing pitches. Its .5-semitone agreement, 1-semitone core-spread, .35-semitone centre and 50% supported-frame coverage tolerances are conservative **triage heuristics**, not universal acoustic/style thresholds. Flagged brief/expressive gestures remain unresolved, not declared noise. Supported gestures keep exact boundaries, including 40ms pickups, with no gap joining or duration floor.

The output's `gestures` retain supported **continuous** `midiFloat` pitch and exact supplied boundaries, even when a stable vowel lies between piano keys. `pianoDecisionRequired` and `adjacentPianoKeys` describe the remaining piano choice; they do not make it. The conservative `notes` and `unresolved` outputs remain compatible. A between-key gesture may be recovered acoustically while its piano target remains unresolved. Do not erase it from phrase coverage or shift the entire recording's tuning to force that vowel into a key. Record an explicit, source-bound piano decision separately, including reduction of inflection and conflicting note-model evidence. Agreement of two trackers is not proof of intended pitch, role or attack. Speech, bleed, unknown role, sparse support, unstable cores and octave disagreements do not enter `gestures`.

Do not redirect a failed repair over a previous good result. Run `python3 scripts/recover_vocal.py --self-test`, then replay actual contrasting excerpts. Synthetic checks establish timing/abstention behavior only.

## Arrangement and delivery

Choose piano pitch/register, attack, release and dynamics after recovery; document deliberate reduction of inflections. Preserve recognizable contour, repetitions, pickups, rests and sustains. No blanket quantization, chord snapping, minimum duration or forced legato. Reuse existing MIDI/MusicXML writers with explicit **Right Hand / Left Hand** labels; verify actual imported lanes and native timing.

Review coverage **by complete native phrase**, not by the number of supported notes. A few correct isolated vowels can still leave an unrecognizable piano line. Account for each proposed gesture as rendered, explicitly reduced, superseded by a source-supported proposal, or unresolved; distinguish acoustic guard warnings from passages actually omitted after explicit piano decisions. Compare with the previous preferred candidate and preserve its decisions unless new source evidence warrants a change. Inspect whole-gesture coverage/spread and core fraction before choosing a plateau; include expressive voiced movement, and record sensitivity when a nearby core boundary changes a triage result. A passing core does not establish intended pitch. Corroborate repeated attacks with native onset/phoneme evidence; reject compressed ASR syllables and aligned words spanning silence rather than adding or sustaining a note for every word.

Authorized UG Vocal notation supports lyric/note correspondence, ties/rests and intended contour. Verify actual saved pixels and sounding octave separately. Align multiple phrase-local anchors to the requested performance; studio BPM/global scaling is insufficient. A blank screenshot is no evidence. Different live pitches/timing stay different unless a source-led arrangement decision resolves them.

If reliable recovery is unavailable, deliver an explicitly labeled **instrumental Original fallback**, with supported hooks/backing and named unrecovered vocal regions. An incomplete vocal sketch is not a full repair. Chords excludes all vocal events. Preserve approved/live files; private diagnostics do not authorize publication.

Render old/new vocal-only and combined excerpts on identical native windows with the same sampled piano, measured pairwise level matching and clipping/decode checks. Surface source/stem excerpts. Verify fresh/idempotent import and Player lanes, seek, loops, practice/wait, notation, transpose and release. State accept/reject separately from structural success. Verify actual audio transport/consumption before claiming model listening; missing listening/keyboard evidence keeps musical readiness rejected/provisional.

Map owner feedback against the **exact preview asset and its native offset** before editing: preview0:10 in a native10–30s clip means source0:20. A source-supported continuous vowel can still have a wrong piano target; use the phrase and actual listening feedback to resolve the adjacent-key choice, not just a learned pitch label. An isolated supported fragment after an unrecovered phrase can sound misplaced even when its pitch passes triage. Omit it explicitly until the phrase is reliably recovered, without applying a global short-note filter; preserve the rest of the preferred candidate.
