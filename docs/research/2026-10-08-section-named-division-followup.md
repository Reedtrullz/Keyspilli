## Eligibility and correspondence

A separately specified study selected these fifteen exact companion-matched
scores before constructing candidates. Each score prints a named division of a
larger work or collection. A standalone genre, tempo, number or unqualified work
title is insufficient. The printed hierarchies are The Lancer's Quadrilles / La
Native (#3) or Les Graces (#4); Peer Gynt Suite I Op46 / its fourth movement;
Suite Bergamasque / Clair de Lune; Carmen / Prelude or Entr'acte (Act II);
Recuerdos de viaje / No.6 Rumores de la Caleta; Giselle / Pas de deux (first act);
Deux Arabesques / Première Arabesque; and six named Aylesford Pieces by Handel.
The Aylesford relationship identifies collection membership and does not assert
that these files form a continuous suite or establish measured fugue structure.

Each opening is independently located by a complete printed pickup or first-bar
pitch/rhythm landmark. Exact companion/source byte identity and all 10,833 actual
producer pitched attacks are checked against distinct raw MIDI attacks, retaining
pitch multiplicity. All match within 1/16 quarter beat, maximum error 0.059896.
Only the retained encoded traversal is followed: no unencoded repeat, D.C./D.S.
occurrence, recording clock or performance itinerary is supplied. Grieg's separate
integrated-tempo study failed and remains excluded; its source-quarter-beat study
passes independently.
