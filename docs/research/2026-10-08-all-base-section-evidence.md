## Evidence methods

Exact FF06/FF07 form markers use `source` after an independent raw-tick/tempo clock check. Unknown or mixed labels end the preceding certainty; they are not swallowed into the preceding named passage. Backstreet Boys' retained upload contributes six bounded Verse/Chorus/Bridge spans; mixed `Chorus t/Br`, `Verse spec` and `Verse +1` intervals remain estimated.

External score annotations use `chart`, even when the score's companion MIDI exactly matches the retained MIDI. Source score hashes and URLs, explicit annotations, raw key/meter events, source note sequences and producer onset correspondence support the mapping. This is distinct from the source-marker tier. Brahms provides four explicitly concatenated movements (Andante, Scherzo, Adagio mesto and Finale), with the actual2.5-beat inter-movement padding left estimated; Beethoven and Haydn provide Menuetto/Trio boundaries; Chopin explicitly marks Coda; Blumenthal explicitly marks Thema/Variation/Finale; Doane explicitly marks Refrain. Maps follow the traversal actually encoded in the retained MIDI. Printed repeat and da-capo directions do not create unencoded occurrences. Padded trailing bars remain estimated where they extend beyond the retained source.

Harmony alignment retains the0.15 ambiguity threshold in eight-bar-equivalent cost units. Mad World's Intro has a0.225 boundary gap and0.7125 local confidence. Its exact retained MIDI has accompaniment from beat0, a distinct main line beginning at16, and an independent source-to-producer onset correspondence check. The repeated interior harmonies remain ambiguous and are not published.

## Bounded lyric excerpts

This method identifies a passage within a chart-named section, not the complete form boundary. Three alternatives were considered: a single first-line anchor (insufficient; often late or a pickup), forcing harmony DP with lyric landmarks (would disguise a weak cost gap), and exact full multi-line text correspondence. The third can establish a bounded excerpt without changing harmony gates.

Accept only an entire chart section's collected lyric paragraph of at least60 normalized characters across at least two substantive lines. Require a unique complete match in the exact retained MIDI's timed FF05 lyric stream. Repeated full paragraphs, conflicting chart labels or overlapping candidate spans remain unresolved. Source quarter beats must independently correspond to pinned producer note onsets throughout the piece. Exact lyric events define the first and last cue onsets; the last syllable's release is not established. Thus labels explicitly say `excerpt`, start at the first matched cue and stop at the last matched cue. They do not imply a downbeat, complete Verse/Chorus/Bridge, recording timestamp or timed source form marker. No sung text is copied into the public map.

The reported confidence is the minimum of full normalized text coverage (1 for exact accepted matches), uniqueness (1 only for a unique match) and independent source-to-producer clock match ratio at1/16-beat tolerance. It is an uncalibrated correspondence score, not a musical probability or acceptance score. Full paragraphs must match without fuzzy repairs or omitted mismatching lines. Repeated matching text is rejected regardless of how convenient a chronology might appear.

Ruling: the owner's autonomous handoff already authorizes chart extraction, alignment decisions and normal green-CI publication. This bounded evidence method needs a reviewed concrete result, not another permission menu. If the interpretation is wrong, the cost is a reversible partial section map; full section boundaries still remain visibly uncertain.

## Acceptance

Every new map is pinned to the exact production source fingerprint, Advanced notes SHA-256 and playback BPM. Source or chart names do not establish artist authority, hearing, recognizability, playability or musical acceptance. Naming is not complete while any base still has only estimates; completing acquisition attempts or rejecting an alignment does not complete that base's naming.
